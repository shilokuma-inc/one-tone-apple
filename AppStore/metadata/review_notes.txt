2. Purpose and target audience
OneTone is a simple tone generator. It plays a steady tone at any frequency from 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz, with a choice of four waveforms (Sine, Square, Triangle, Sawtooth). It can also play a tone by note name (NOTE mode, e.g. A4) or a three-note major or minor chord (CHORD mode, e.g. C or Am).
- Purpose: to provide a constant, distraction-free tone that helps users focus or relax while working or studying, and a quick reference tone for checking speakers or headphones, tuning instruments (e.g. 440 Hz), hearing how notes and chords sound, and learning how pitch and waveform affect sound.
- Target audience: students and people who want a background tone to concentrate, musicians, and audio hobbyists.
- Value: it starts in one tap with no setup, works fully offline, and shows the actual output on an oscilloscope-style waveform display and a level meter.

3. How to use the app
No login, account, setup or sample files are required. All features are available immediately after launch.
1) Launch the app. On the first launch, a short tutorial opens automatically. Use Next to read it or Skip to close it. It can be reopened at any time with the "?" button at the top left.
2) The main screen shows the waveform display, the OUTPUT panel and the FREQUENCY panel. Tap PLAY to start the tone. Tap STOP to stop it.
3) Change the frequency with the knob, the slider, the number field (enter a value in Hz and press Return/Done) or the preset buttons (100 Hz / 440 Hz / 1 kHz / 10 kHz). The tone changes while playing.
4) Select a waveform: Sine, Square, Triangle or Sawtooth.
5) Adjust the VOLUME fader. The level meter and the waveform display follow the actual output.
6) Open Settings with the gear button at the top right (on macOS 13, use Settings… in the app menu). Settings contains:
- FREQUENCY MODE: Hz (default), NOTE or CHORD. In NOTE mode, the FREQUENCY panel selects a note such as A4 and the knob and slider move in semitones. In CHORD mode, it shows chord pads (C, G, Am, ...) and a root / Major / Minor selector, and PLAY plays the three notes of the chord.
- THEME COLOR: the accent color of the screen (8 colors).
- APP ICON (iPhone and iPad only): an alternate app icon in each theme color.
- LICENSES: the license of the bundled logo typeface.
- RESET: restores the sound settings and the mode to their defaults.
Note: the app remembers the last frequency, volume, waveform and mode. If the remembered volume is above 20%, it is lowered to 20% at launch and an alert explains why, to avoid a sudden loud sound. Tones near 20 Hz or 20,000 Hz may be hard to hear depending on the speakers and the listener. Please start at a moderate volume.

4. External services
None. The app does not use any external services, SDKs, servers, authentication, payment processors, analytics, advertising or AI services. It does not connect to the network. Sound is generated entirely on the device with Apple's AVFoundation (AVAudioEngine).

5. Regional differences
None. The app functions and displays the same content in all regions. The user interface is localized into 12 languages (English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian and Indonesian) and follows the device language.

6. Regulated industries / third-party material
Not applicable. The app does not operate in a regulated industry and is not a medical or hearing-test device. All sounds are synthesized by the app. The only third-party material is the Audiowide typeface used in the logo, which is bundled under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the full license text is shown in Settings > LICENSES.
